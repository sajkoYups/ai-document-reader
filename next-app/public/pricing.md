# PIA pricing

Private Mode is free forever. Deep Analysis is a paid, waitlist feature for higher-volume and team use.

## Private Mode

- Price: €0 / month
- The AI runs entirely in your browser
- Document never leaves your device
- No account required
- No usage limits
- Document cleared on page refresh

## Deep Analysis

A stronger server-side model. PIA encrypts the request to its API and does not store documents or prompts. Providers are listed in the Trust Center.

| Plan | Price | Volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lite | €9 / month | 10 analyses / month |
| Starter | €29 / month | 75 analyses / month |
| Pro | €79 / month | 200 analyses / month |
| Business | €299 / month | 500+ analyses / month |

Analyses are counted by token usage internally. Very long documents may count as more than one analysis. Exact usage is visible in the account dashboard.
